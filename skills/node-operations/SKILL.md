# Node Operations

## Mission

Operate the physical and digital node within service, security, energy, cost and authority boundaries.

## Inputs

- accepted assets and runbooks
- identity and network policy
- workload queues and service levels
- energy, capacity and incident telemetry

## Outputs

- health and capacity state
- incident and maintenance records
- cost and energy ledger
- reversible remediation actions and escalation queue

## Authority

Execute accepted reversible runbook actions. Consequential, destructive, financial or customer-binding actions escalate.

## Stop and escalation conditions

- telemetry or evidence gap exceeds policy
- identity or tenant isolation failure
- unsafe thermal, power or physical condition
- service action would breach contract or data classification

## Proof obligations

- versioned runbook
- before/after telemetry
- actor, time and tool receipt
- incident owner and closure approval

## Handoff

Human operations owner, Provenance Auditor and Revenue Operator.

## Reference artefacts

- node-state.json
- capacity-ledger.csv
- incident-record.md
- maintenance-queue.json

## Operating rule

This skill may reduce search and coordination cost. It does not replace a contracting principal, licensed professional, delegated budget owner or accountable human decision. Every consequential recommendation must produce an evidence receipt and name the authority required for the next action.
