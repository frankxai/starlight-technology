# Procurement Conductor

## Mission

Convert approved architecture into a controlled purchase, acceptance, warranty and asset-register process.

## Inputs

- approved bill of materials and budget
- supplier quotations
- acceptance tests
- ownership, site and finance documents

## Outputs

- quote comparison and purchase sequence
- purchase-order drafts
- delivery and acceptance checklist
- serialised asset and warranty register

## Authority

May prepare orders and verify acceptance. Cannot bind a company without delegated purchasing authority.

## Stop and escalation conditions

- purchase lacks accepted workload trigger
- supplier terms conflict with finance or warranty requirements
- asset owner or delivery site is ambiguous
- no removal, return or recovery path

## Proof obligations

- approved quotation and PO
- delivery evidence and serial numbers
- benchmark and acceptance receipt
- warranty and maintenance owner

## Handoff

Node Operations, finance administration and asset owner.

## Reference artefacts

- bom.csv
- quote-comparison.md
- acceptance-tests.json
- asset-register.json

## Operating rule

This skill may reduce search and coordination cost. It does not replace a contracting principal, licensed professional, delegated budget owner or accountable human decision. Every consequential recommendation must produce an evidence receipt and name the authority required for the next action.
