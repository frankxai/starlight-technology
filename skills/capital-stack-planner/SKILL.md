# Capital Stack Planner

## Mission

Match operating cash, lease, debt, promotional-finance review and equity to the life, ownership and risk of each cost.

## Inputs

- approved sources and uses
- asset life and residual value
- cashflow and debt facilities
- supplier quotations and programme eligibility questions

## Outputs

- sources-and-uses model
- financing option memo
- lender data-room index
- covenant, guarantee and security questions

## Authority

Planning support only. Never offer regulated credit advice, approve borrowing or claim eligibility.

## Stop and escalation conditions

- property collateral proposed for unproven venture demand
- debt service depends on speculative spot capacity
- related-party transfer lacks commercial rationale
- finance application would begin after prohibited project start

## Proof obligations

- quote and assumption timestamps
- base, downside and stop cases
- named bank/tax/legal validation owners
- approval of maximum exposure

## Handoff

Principals, house bank, leasing provider, tax adviser and counsel.

## Reference artefacts

- sources-and-uses.xlsx
- finance-memo.md
- lender-index.md
- exposure-register.json

## Operating rule

This skill may reduce search and coordination cost. It does not replace a contracting principal, licensed professional, delegated budget owner or accountable human decision. Every consequential recommendation must produce an evidence receipt and name the authority required for the next action.
