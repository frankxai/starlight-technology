# Product Strategy

## Position

**Starlight Technology is buying intelligence and complete-system design for AI-native creators and small teams.** The launch wedge is the **AI Creator Studio**.

## Portfolio boundary

- `frankx.ai/stack`: Frank's personal owned/used/rejected narrative and distribution bridge.
- `starlight.technology`: market-wide decision models, comparisons, builds, offers and later alerts.
- `starlightintelligence.org`: open intelligence substrate for agents, memory, governance and evaluation.
- `starlightelligence.ai`: planned premium applied-intelligence system product.

No duplicated full articles across domains. Cross-domain references point to one canonical page.

## Sequence

1. Evidence-rich editorial and deterministic decision starter.
2. Human-reviewed Creator System Blueprint.
3. Structured product/offer graph after repeated demand.
4. Merchant feeds and alerts only with authorised data use.
5. AI buyer only as an explanation layer over approved records.
