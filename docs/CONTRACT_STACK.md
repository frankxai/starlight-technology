# Infrastructure Partnership Contract Stack

This document is a legal issue map, not legal advice or an executable template. Each transaction requires counsel in the relevant jurisdictions.

## Stage 0 — permission to learn

1. Mutual NDA and data-room protocol.
2. Fixed-fee Phase 0 statement of work.
3. Named principals, reliance boundary, exclusions and acceptance method.

## Pilot — permission to build

4. Master services agreement and implementation SOW.
5. Data-processing, security and acceptable-use schedules.
6. IP, software, skill and client-specific artefact schedule.
7. Authority, approvals, change control and incident schedule.

## Physical deployment — permission to occupy and operate

8. Site / host / colocation agreement.
9. Equipment purchase, lease, warranty and asset schedule.
10. Energy EPC, O&M, storage, metering or supply instrument.
11. Capacity, offtake or managed-service agreement.
12. Insurance, maintenance, access, removal and recovery schedule.

## Expansion — permission to leverage

13. Loan, lease, guarantees, collateral and covenant documents.
14. Shareholder or project-company documents where a NodeCo is justified.
15. Related-party, transfer-pricing, VAT and cross-border service memorandum where relevant.
16. Step-in, deadlock, exit, transition and data-portability instruments.

## Mandatory commercial questions

- Which legal entity buys each asset and receives each invoice?
- Which entity receives the business benefit and carries the operating obligation?
- What is fixed price, usage price, energy pass-through, capacity reservation and performance fee?
- What rights exist only while capital or guarantee exposure remains outstanding?
- Which rights expire automatically, and what evidence triggers release?
- Who controls priorities during scarce capacity or incidents?
- Who owns data, generic platform IP, client-specific workflows and each class of output?
- Which system is authoritative for price, inventory, finance and customer commitments?
- How does either party remove assets, export data and continue operations after termination?
- What cannot be delegated to an agent or workflow?
