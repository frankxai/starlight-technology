# Single-operator licence (SOL-1) — draft

Draft published for review on 2026-10-16. Not yet applied to any purchase. Final text is fixed with the merchant-of-record setup before checkout opens.

**Grant.** One named operator may install and run the Workflow Edition on machines and private infrastructure they control, modify its templates, prompts and checks for their own use, and ship briefs and derived artifacts to clients without attribution.

**Updates.** Twelve months of updates within the major version from the purchase date. Every version received remains licensed.

**Restrictions.** No redistribution or resale of the workflow, templates or checklists; no hosting the workflow as a service for third parties; one licence per operator.

**Warranty and support.** Defects in the shipped files are repaired or refunded. Support scope and response targets are stated on the product page. No warranty that a model configured by the operator will read sources correctly; the review gate exists for that reason.

**Consumer rights.** EU consumers retain the 14-day withdrawal right until they request delivery to begin and acknowledge that the right then lapses; checkout records that consent. Statutory rights for defective digital content are unaffected.
