# ADR 0001: Domain and platform boundary

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-19

## Decision

Use `starlight.technology` as the canonical buying-intelligence product. Keep `frankx.ai/stack` as the founder's personal authority surface and distribution bridge. Do not create a competing full publication at `frankx.ai/technology`.

Architect the data model for multiple categories, but expose only routes with evidence-rich content. The first campaign is AI Creator Studio.

## Consequences

- One canonical URL per analysis.
- FrankX may explain personal use and link to Starlight decisions.
- Starlight Intelligence protocol/product surfaces retain separate missions.
- Product graph, merchant feeds, deals and AI buyer are later stages.
