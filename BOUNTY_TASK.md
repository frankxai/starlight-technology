# Bounty Objective: Validate AI Creator Studio buyers, digital offers, affiliate economics and community contribution
Target: frankxai/starlight-technology Issue #31
URL: https://github.com/frankxai/starlight-technology/issues/31

## Issue Description
## Outcome

Identify a paying initial audience and a repeatable self-service offer from actual completed workflows. Keep Starlight Technology's accepted buying-intelligence mission and AI Creator Studio launch wedge. Proposed purpose: help independent builders use intelligence to create useful work they can own, improve and share.

## Discovery and community plan

Initial hypotheses: independent software builders/technical founders, small software/creative studios, and local-model researchers. Later: educators/maker groups and less technical creators. Distinguish useful contributors from likely paying customers. Validate their last real delayed/failed task, current workaround, purchase timing, useful output and switching barrier.

Prepare a six-week pilot with ten consented workflow observations, at least five in the technical-builder wedge. Target five outside users independently completing the promised workflow and three demonstrating willingness to pay at a stated price, with zero critical rights/security/permission failures. Ratify those decision thresholds before execution; they are small discovery aims, not proof of general market size. Retain dropouts and assistance needed.

Map candidate channels: GitHub/OpenAI developer community for tool compatibility, Ollama/llama.cpp/Hugging Face for local-model methods, Indie Hackers for product economics, and Vercel/Next.js for web engineering. Verify actual community rules and audience fit before any participation. Prepare substantial field notes with reusable evidence/artifacts; posting, messages and event/community applications require their applicable authorization.

Use the existing owned site/demand capture first. Proposed community practice: attributed reproducible build logs, failure notes and peer improvements. Define consent, contribution rights, moderation, private-data removal and sponsor independence. Open a new community channel only when repeated participation warrants maintenance. Preserve existing community/content systems.

## Revenue experiments

- Free capacity diagnostic and editable example lead to the relevant paid-product demand form.
- Test proposed one-time digital-kit bands of EUR 49/99/149 and team-license bands of EUR 199/499 as research questions. Specify contents and license; do not publish checkout promises before release.
- Assess affiliate programme approval and regional terms. Show all suitable merchants/alternatives; commission must not change ranking. Verify disclosure and attribution rules through current authoritative guidance before implementation.
- Continuing benchmark/library access, specialist packs and institutional curriculum follow demonstrated repeat utility. Avoid individual consulting, managed support obligations and undefined membership value.
- Later sponsorship/co-marketing requires editorial separation. Partner integration is not endorsement.

## Market and economics

Build bottom-up TAM/SAM/reachable-market scenarios with dated sources and explicit overlap/eligibility assumptions. Do not add developer, GitHub and local-model population totals. Initial reachable buyers = unique qualified reach × plan activation × willingness to pay × actual conversion, with each stage measured separately.

Report gross receipts separately from VAT, fees, refunds, maintenance, acquisition and operating contribution. Proposed EUR 99 × 10/50/100 sales gives EUR 990/4,950/9,900 gross, solely arithmetic. Minisforum's checked global page advertises 2% qualified-sales commission and a 30-day cookie; EU eligibility, approval and payouts remain unknown. EUR 4,000 × 2% is EUR 80 before deductions, not evidence of profitable acquisition.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Evidence-backed segment/job ranking and serious current alternatives; no fabricated interviews, demand, endorsements or market totals.
- [ ] Approved research instrument, channel/rules map, privacy/rights plan and registered pilot thresholds.
- [ ] Completed-use and willingness-to-pay evidence with denominators, assistance and rejected outcomes; independent buyer/domain review.
- [ ] Offer/license/fulfillment and demand-form mapping reconciles deployed product truth. Affiliate programme/cookie/commission/region verified before activation.
- [ ] Unit economics scenarios expose uncertainty, channel attribution, refunds and maintenance burden. Recurring plans require retained-use evidence.
- [ ] Narrative and visual examples receive refinement and exact-revision review. Every factual promise links to evidence; broader Starlight ambition is labeled vision.
